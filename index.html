About

I got into troubleshooting through Cisco networking — CCNA labs, routing tables, the discipline of tracing a problem to its exact cause. I brought that same approach to Google Merchant Center.

Since 2020 I've worked with WordPress and Shopify store owners whose GMC accounts were suspended, flagged for misrepresentation, or stuck with disapproved products they couldn't explain. Most of the time the fix isn't guesswork — it's reading the policy correctly, auditing the feed line by line, and correcting what Google is actually objecting to, not what looks wrong at a glance.

Alongside GMC work, I still keep my CCNA networking skills sharp — static and dynamic routing, VLSM subnetting, and Packet Tracer labs are below as proof of that background.

Services

GMC Misrepresentation Fix

Account flagged or suspended for misrepresentation. I audit your store, landing pages, and checkout flow against Google's policy, fix what's actually non-compliant, and submit a reinstatement request that addresses the real issue.

WordPress & Shopify Feed Sync

Product Approval & Policy Fix
